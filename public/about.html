Who is Wahab?

One winter night. One faint light. One goal the world called impossible. Wahab Zafar is the founder and creator of PRIMEPIPFX, the Prime Pip SBT Indicator and the Prime Pip FX Command Center. Life knocked him down three times, with three serious injuries, and he never lost his resolve. Along the way he built something so unexpected that people could not even imagine it, and many more features are coming.

Chapter 1 – The Beginning

Wahab was born in a small, rural area of Jhang, Pakistan, in a place where resources were limited and access to global financial markets was nonexistent. His family lived through very difficult economic times. He was not born into an extraordinary life, so he resolved to build one himself, and refused to let geography or hardship define his destiny.

Chapter 2 – The Battles

Three severe injuries tested his mental and physical strength:

Age 6 – a year housebound

An iron door latch tore away a large piece of flesh from his left leg and caused a severe fracture. He was confined to bed for an entire year, and all he prayed for was to be able to run again.

Age 16 – two years unable to walk

Passionate about sports, he suffered another serious fracture, this time in the knee of the same left leg, while playing football. It took two hard years of recovery to walk again, and it ended his athletic ambitions.

Before university – a chronic back injury

Driven by enthusiasm for fitness but without guidance, he lifted too much weight during squats and badly injured his lower back. It became a chronic condition that he still manages today.

Hardship at home added pressure to these setbacks. Rather than breaking his resolve it built discipline. He realised nobody was coming to save them, and that he had to forge his own path to independence through technology and trading.

Chapter 3 – The Turning Point

When Wahab found the financial markets he knew he had found his way forward. The first 18 months were a trial of fire: blown accounts, psychological strain and overwhelming noise. The turning point came when he sought out his mentor, Sir Hamza of Faisalabad, who taught strict risk management, psychological composure and statistical discipline. He moved from emotional speculation to a measurable edge.

Chapter 4 – The Breakthrough

With firm risk management and refined models, his results changed. He passed demanding account evaluations, earned funded trader status and began trading consistently. The breakthrough showed that grit, honest mentorship and strict discipline can overcome any obstacle.

Chapter 5 – The Creation

Personal success was only the first step. Wahab created the Prime Pip SBT Indicator, the first iteration of the SBT Bot. It scans 10 institutional models (SBT 1 through SBT 10B), market structure shifts (MSS) and liquidity boundaries. The upcoming autonomous SBT Bot builds on this framework, and everything is unified inside the Prime Pip FX Command Center.

Chapter 6 – The Vision

Prime Pip FX is growing through its YouTube channel, its verified student community and continuous technology work. Wahab's aim is that anyone, no matter how humble their beginnings, can have access to the tools, discipline and opportunity to reach real freedom.
