The founder

PRIMEPIPFX was created by Wahab. Life gave him three injuries, and he never gave up. Instead he built something the world did not expect, something beyond what most people could imagine. Many more features are coming in the future.

Everything in one application

The terminal tells you which currency pair to trade and puts everything a trader needs in a single app:

  • Fundamental data for forex pairs, gold (XAUUSD) and other markets
  • Backtesting tools to test ideas on past prices
  • Unlimited charts and a premium TradingView subscription experience
  • Freehand canvas workspace for drawing and planning trades
  • Prime Pip SBT indicator and trading terminal packages
  • Community, 1-on-1 messaging and video calling for students
  • Psychological Command Center for trading discipline
  • Every category is clearly laid out, with a detailed description of what each one contains
